Turing's Cat: The AI Researcher Who Turned Complex Technology into Bilibili's Most Accessible Science

Baby Zhao October 9, 2026

Turing's Cat, known in Chinese as Tuling de Mao, is a Chinese artificial intelligence researcher, technology entrepreneur, and one of Bilibili's most respected science and technology content creators. The account was founded in 2019 by Wang Yiran, a researcher with an academic background spanning the Chinese University of Hong Kong and professional experience as an engineer at Amazon. The name is a deliberate intellectual homage, combining the surname of Alan Turing, the father of theoretical computer science and artificial intelligence, with a reference to Schrödinger's cat, the famous thought experiment in quantum mechanics. This dual reference captures the essence of the channel's mission: to make the most complex and abstract technologies of the twenty-first century accessible, tangible, and relevant to ordinary people. As of January 2025, the Bilibili channel has 1.085 million followers, and Wang Yiran's total following across all platforms exceeds 2.1 million. He was named to Bilibili's annual Top 100 UP Creator list in 2024 and again in 2026 for the 2025 list, recognition that places him among the platform's most influential voices in science and technology communication. He is also the founder of Creaibo, an AI-powered content creation tool developed by his company, which has been presented at industry events in Shanghai's Yangpu District as an example of the city's digital economy innovation.

Wang Yiran's path to becoming one of China's leading AI communicators was not a straight line from academia to media. He began his research career at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, where he studied artificial intelligence and developed the technical foundation that would later inform his public communication. He then worked as an engineer at Amazon, gaining practical experience in the technology industry and observing firsthand how AI systems were being deployed at scale. In 2019, while still working in the technology sector, he began uploading AI-related content to Bilibili, initially as a side project. At that time, artificial intelligence was still a relatively remote concept for most Chinese internet users, associated with science fiction and academic research rather than everyday life. Wang Yiran recognized that the gap between what AI researchers understood and what the general public knew was vast, and that the rapid pace of technological change was making that gap increasingly consequential. His first videos were explanatory and accessible, designed to demystify AI concepts without sacrificing accuracy. Over the following years, as AI moved from a niche academic subject to a dominant force in global technology and economics, his channel grew alongside the public's appetite for understanding it.

What distinguishes Turing's Cat from other technology creators on Bilibili is the depth of technical expertise that Wang Yiran brings to his content and his willingness to engage with AI at the level of research and development rather than merely reviewing consumer products. His videos cover topics ranging from the architecture of large language models and the mechanics of transformer networks to the practical applications of AI in content creation, scientific research, and everyday productivity. He has produced detailed analyses of major AI releases from companies such as OpenAI, Google, and Chinese developers, evaluating their capabilities and limitations with the rigor of someone who understands the underlying technology rather than merely reporting on marketing claims. His video on the Chinese AI model DeepSeek, released in early 2025, examined the technical innovations that allowed the model to achieve competitive performance at a fraction of the training cost of comparable Western systems, and it became one of his most widely viewed works, accumulating millions of views and generating extensive discussion across Chinese social media. The video's success reflected a broader moment in which Chinese audiences were hungry for informed, non-sensational analysis of their country's position in the global AI race.

Among his most significant contributions is the "AI Detailed Explanation" series, which functions as a structured curriculum for viewers who want to understand artificial intelligence from first principles. The series begins with the mathematical foundations of machine learning, moves through the history of neural network research, and culminates in detailed examinations of contemporary systems such as GPT, BERT, and diffusion models. Wang Yiran's teaching style is patient and methodical, and he frequently uses analogies and visual aids to make abstract concepts concrete. He does not assume prior technical knowledge, but he also does not simplify to the point of inaccuracy. This balance has made his content valuable to a wide range of viewers, from university students studying computer science to professionals in other fields who need to understand AI for their work. His videos on AI-generated content, including images, video, and music, have been particularly influential, providing both technical explanations and critical perspectives on the ethical and social implications of these technologies.

Turing's Cat's platform achievements reflect both his technical authority and his communication skill. His selection to Bilibili's Top 100 UP Creator list in 2024 and 2025 places him among a select group of technology creators recognized for sustained excellence. His follower count of over one million on Bilibili, while smaller than that of some entertainment creators, represents a highly engaged and technically literate audience that includes researchers, developers, entrepreneurs, and students. His company, Creaibo, has developed an AI creation tool that has been showcased at industry events and positioned as a practical application of the technologies he explains in his videos. In 2025, he participated in the Yangpu V-Park initiative, a Shanghai government-supported program designed to foster digital economy innovation, where he presented his team's work on AI-assisted content creation. His influence extends beyond Bilibili: his analyses are cited in Chinese technology media, and his videos are frequently referenced in discussions of AI policy, education, and industry development.

The broader social value of Wang Yiran's work lies in his role as a bridge between the frontier of AI research and the general public. In a period of rapid technological change, when AI systems are transforming industries, reshaping labor markets, and raising profound questions about creativity, privacy, and human agency, the ability of ordinary people to understand these technologies is not merely a matter of intellectual curiosity but of informed citizenship. Wang Yiran has built his audience by treating his viewers as capable of engaging with complex ideas, and his success demonstrates that there is a substantial demand for serious, accurate, and accessible AI education in China. His work has particular significance for young people considering careers in technology, who can use his videos as a supplement to formal education and as a guide to the skills and knowledge that will be most valuable in an AI-driven economy. At the same time, he has not shied away from addressing the risks and uncertainties associated with AI, including questions of misinformation, job displacement, and the concentration of technological power. His willingness to engage with these issues without resorting to either techno-utopianism or alarmism reflects an intellectual integrity that distinguishes his work from much of the hype-driven commentary that surrounds AI.

In summary, Turing's Cat represents a model of the creator as a public technologist, someone who has used the skills and knowledge acquired through academic research and industry experience to help a mass audience navigate the most consequential technological shift of the present era. Wang Yiran's journey from an AI researcher at the Chinese University of Hong Kong and Amazon to a leading voice in Chinese science communication reflects a deliberate choice to prioritize public understanding over purely academic or commercial pursuits. His one million followers on Bilibili, his two Top 100 UP Creator selections, and his recognition by Shanghai's digital economy initiatives are all evidence that this choice has been both intellectually and professionally successful. For Bilibili, he is a cornerstone of the platform's technology section and a demonstration that knowledge content can achieve both popularity and rigor. For viewers, he offers a reminder that the most important technologies are not those we use without thinking but those we take the time to understand. And for the broader landscape of Chinese digital culture, his career is a testament to the possibility of building a meaningful and influential public role by doing the hard work of explaining difficult things clearly.
